Glossary

Block

The basic piece of the puzzle. A block's color and height decide how its answer relates to its hint.

Blocks can be stacked, placed one behind another in a chain, or sit side by side.

Prompt

A block that has a puzzle on it: a hint and a text field for the answer. "Prompt" and "puzzle block" are used interchangeably.

Height

A block has one of three heights: High, Middle, or Low.

  • High: the rule works on how the word sounds.
  • Middle: the rule works on how the word is spelled.
  • Low: the rule works on what the word means.

Chain

Blocks joined in a horizontal line. Their rules combine, and the answer reflects all of them.
