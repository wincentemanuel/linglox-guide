# Really Red
- hex: #FF0000, rgb: 255, 0, 0
- Meaning: taking away from the hint. The main mechanic is removing
  letters.
- Like: cutting a piece off. What is left is smaller than what you started with.

## High
- Rule: remove part of the hint while keeping the sound of what remains.
  The answer is a homophone of part of the hint.

## Middle
- Rule: remove part of the hint. The remaining letters keep their
  spelling. The answer is found inside the hint.

## Low
- Rule: reduce the hint from a whole to one of its parts, by meaning.
