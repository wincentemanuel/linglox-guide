# Really Black
- hex: #111111, rgb: 17, 17, 17
- Meaning: opposites.
- Like: a mirror image. The hint is flipped around.

## High
- Rule: reverse the order of the sounds in the hint. The answer is a word
  that sounds like the reversed result (homophone).

## Middle
- Rule: reverse the spelling of the hint, letter by letter.
- If the hint is a palindrome, the answer is the same word.

## Low
- Rule: the answer is an antonym of the hint.
