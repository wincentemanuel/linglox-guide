# New Yeller
- hex: #FFFF00, rgb: 255, 255, 0
- Meaning: mixing up or scrambling the hint.
- Like: shuffling a deck of cards. The same pieces, in a different order.

## High
- Rule: move the syllables of the hint around. The spelling is not
  necessarily kept.

## Middle
- Rule: the answer is an anagram of the hint.

## Low
- Rule: the answer is the result of physically scrambling or taking
  apart what the hint names.
