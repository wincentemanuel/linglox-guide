Really Blue

Hex: #0000FF
RGB: 0, 0, 255

In Linglox, a puzzle block shows a hint word and asks you to type a related word back. Really Blue means adding to the hint by adding letters.

Like: Adding to a list. The hint stays, and more is added to it.

High High

The answer contains a word that sounds like the hint, without necessarily keeping the original spelling (homophone).

Example:

see
season

Middle Middle

Add letters to the hint while keeping the original letters in their exact spelling.

Example:

plan
planet

Low Low

Build the hint up from an individual part to a larger whole by meaning.

Example:

wheel
car
