Really Red

Hex: #FF0000
RGB: 255, 0, 0

In Linglox, a puzzle block shows a hint word and asks you to type a related word back. Really Red means taking away from the hint by removing letters.

Like: Cutting a piece off. What is left is smaller than what you started with.

High High

Remove part of the hint while keeping the sound of what remains, forming a word that sounds identical (homophone).

Example:

bear
air

Middle Middle

Remove part of the hint so the remaining letters keep their spelling to reveal an answer found inside the hint.

Example:

train
rain

Low Low

Reduce the hint from a whole to one of its parts by meaning.

Example:

car
wheel
