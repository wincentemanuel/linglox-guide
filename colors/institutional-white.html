Institutional White

Hex: #F8F8F8
RGB: 248, 248, 248

In Linglox, a puzzle block shows a hint word and asks you to type a related word back. Institutional White means keeping something about the word the same.

Like: A request for a duplicate. The answer has to match the hint in one specific way.

High High

Type a word that sounds the same out loud, even if spelled differently (homophone).

Example:

sea
see

Middle Middle

Type the exact same word back, letter for letter.

Example:

echo
echo

Low Low

Type a different word with a similar meaning (synonym).

Example:

start
begin
