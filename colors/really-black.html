Really Black

Hex: #111111
RGB: 17, 17, 17

In Linglox, a puzzle block shows a hint word and asks you to type a related word back. Really Black means opposites.

Like: A mirror image. The hint is flipped around.

High High

Reverse the order of the sounds in the hint so the answer sounds like the reversed result (homophone).

Example:

tap
pat

Middle Middle

Reverse the spelling of the hint, letter by letter. If the hint reads the same backward and forward (palindrome), the answer is the same word.

Example:

star
rats

Low Low

Type a word that has the opposite meaning of the hint (antonym).

Example:

hot
cold
