New Yeller

Hex: #FFFF00
RGB: 255, 255, 0

In Linglox, a puzzle block shows a hint word and asks you to type a related word back. New Yeller means mixing up or scrambling the hint.

Like: Shuffling a deck of cards. The same pieces, in a different order.

High High

Move the spoken syllables of the hint around, without necessarily keeping the original spelling.

Example:

outbreak
breakout

Middle Middle

Rearrange all the letters of the hint to form a new word (anagram).

Example:

heart
earth

Low Low

Type a word that is the result of physically scrambling or taking apart what the hint names.

Example:

egg
omelet
